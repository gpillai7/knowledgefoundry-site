Reading the graph. Inheritance runs top to bottom and is one-way: a lower level chooses how to satisfy a higher one and may never revise it. KF-3001–3005 decompose the reference architecture into domains; KF-3006 reassembles them into a working system; KF-3007 asks whether that system conforms; KF-3008 and KF-3009 supply the authority model and the exhaustive crosswalks. KF-2003 grades how deep every obligation below it must be discharged. This page is navigational only; each document governs its own content.
